A little about me

Rooted in motherhood.
Inspired by curiosity.

Hi, I'm Elakkiya Soundararajan, a mother of two boys, a certified handwriting expert, and a certified early childhood educator. I've had the joy of guiding 1,500+ children and helping 700+ parents.

My love for teaching and my elder son's natural curiosity inspired our homeschooling journey until he was six, allowing us to learn and explore together.

I'm passionate about learning, reading, journaling, and simplifying concepts. Beyond handwriting, I love empowering parents through homeschooling, money management, and practical time management.

I believe learning never stops, and I love sharing what I learn along the way!

Art & Craft Classes

Art is where my beautiful connection with children first began. Through drawing, art, and craft, I encourage children to explore their imagination and embrace intuitive drawing, where creativity flows freely without the pressure of perfection.
